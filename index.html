How We Build Long-Lasting Pieces

At our Sipara (Patna) workshop, we employ seasoned carpenters who cut, cure and frame premium logs manually. No cheap particle boards or weak hollow sheets are used.

01

100% Seasoned Solid Hardwood

We source real Indian Teak (Sagwan) and Sheesham wood directly. The timber is heat-seasoned to eliminate warping and swelling over time.

02

Traditional Joinery & Reinforcements

Our artisans use deep groove joints and heavy screw mounts instead of cheap surface glue, assuring a life expectancy exceeding 25 years.

03

Polyurethane Stain & Wax Polish

We apply water-resistant lacquer and high-grade wax coatings that prevent staining from spillages and allow effortless dry dusting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of wood do you use?
We primarily build using pure **Sagwan (Teak Wood)**, seasoned **Sheesham (Indian Rosewood)**, and highly durable **Sal Wood** (often used for heavy frame support and double beds). We strictly do not use sawdust boards.
Do you offer delivery across Patna?
Yes. We provide delivery across Patna (including Patna City, Sipara, Kankarbagh, Anisabad, Bailey Road, Saguna More, Danapur) using local transport. Freight charges are calculated based on the travel distance.
Is it possible to customize the sizes?
Absolutely! Since we run our own manufacturing carpentry factory in Patna City, we can customize wood size, cushioning soft fabrics, leatherette shades, and exact dimensions based on your floor layout.
